## How We Evaluate: Our Scoring Methodology

Every vendor is scored across six dimensions. This isn't a perfect science, but it's grounded in real-world experience and cybersecurity vendor selection criteria that matter in practice:

- **Detection Efficacy (25%):** How well does it actually catch threats? Based on independent lab tests (AV-TEST, MITRE ATT&CK evaluations) and our own testing.
- **Ease of Deployment (15%):** How long from purchase to production? Agents, integrations, policy configuration.
- **Operational Overhead (15%):** How much analyst time does it consume daily? False positive rates, console usability, alert quality.
- **Integration Ecosystem (15%):** How well does it play with other tools? API quality, SIEM integration, SOAR playbooks.
- **Total Cost of Ownership (15%):** Licensing, infrastructure, training, and ongoing management costs over 3 years.
- **Vendor Viability (15%):** Financial health, product roadmap, customer retention, support quality.

## Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs) vs. MDR

Managed security service providers and MDR providers serve different needs. MSSPs typically manage your security tools (firewall management, patching, monitoring) while MDR providers focus specifically on threat detection and response with dedicated analyst teams. For most mid-market companies, MDR delivers better security outcomes, while MSSPs are more appropriate if you need help managing infrastructure.

## How to Evaluate Cybersecurity Vendors: Practical Tips

Beyond scores and rankings, here's what actually matters when evaluating vendors:

- Always run a Proof of Concept (PoC) in your environment. Lab tests don't reflect real-world complexity.
- Check customer retention rates, not just new customer growth. High churn is a red flag.
- Ask for references in your industry and size bracket. A tool that works for a 50,000-person bank may not suit a 200-person fintech.
- Calculate true TCO including implementation, training, integration, and ongoing management — not just the license fee.
- Evaluate the vendor's response to recent CVEs and zero-days. How fast did they ship detection content?
