## What is CRM Software?

Customer relationship management software is a cloud application that centralizes every interaction your company has with a prospect, customer, or partner — emails, calls, meetings, quotes, support tickets, renewals — against a single customer record. Where sales used to track deals in spreadsheets, marketing in a separate automation tool, and support in a ticket system, modern CRM software brings all three together so every team sees the full context of the relationship.

### What is CRM software?

Customer relationship management (CRM) software is a cloud application that centralizes every interaction between a company and its prospects, customers, and partners — emails, calls, meetings, deals, support tickets — against a single customer record. It replaces spreadsheets and disconnected tools with a shared system of record for sales, marketing, and service.

### Which CRM is best for small business?

The best CRM for small business is one that deploys in under a week, costs under $50 per user per month, includes mobile and email automation, and doesn't require a dedicated administrator. It should handle contacts, companies, deals, and basic sales automation out of the box — without needing a consulting engagement to set up.

### What is the difference between CRM and customer service software?

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a broader category that includes sales, marketing, and service. Customer service software is typically a sub-module focused on case management, ticketing, and SLA tracking. An all-in-one CRM solution includes customer service as one of several modules sharing a single customer record.

### Can CRM integrate with my ERP and accounting software?

Yes. Modern cloud CRM platforms integrate with ERP and accounting software via native connectors or API. When both CRM and ERP are on the same unified business platform (like Artiflex IT), integration is automatic — a closed deal in CRM creates the sales order in ERP immediately, with no middleware needed.

### How long does CRM implementation take?

Typical CRM implementation timelines: 2–3 weeks for small business with a clean contact import, 6–10 weeks for mid-market with custom fields, sales stages, and email integration, and 3–4 months for enterprise rollouts with multiple teams, territory rules, and integrations to ERP or marketing automation.

### What is CRM with sales automation?

CRM with sales automation adds rule-based workflows on top of basic contact and deal tracking: automatic email sequences, lead scoring and routing, auto-logging of emails and calls, task creation for stalled deals, and quote generation. Sales automation typically saves reps 4–6 hours per week on administrative work.

### Do I need CRM for a service business?

Yes — service businesses benefit more from CRM than many product companies, because repeat visits, recurring revenue, and customer lifetime value are central to the model. A CRM for service businesses adds job tracking, technician dispatch, mobile work orders, and automatic invoicing on job completion.
