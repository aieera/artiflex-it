## What is ERP Software?

Enterprise resource planning software is an integrated application that manages a company's core business processes — finance, procurement, inventory, manufacturing, supply chain, and human resources — from a single shared database. Before ERP, each function ran on its own software, and month-end close was an act of reconciliation. With a modern cloud ERP, the numbers tie out in real time because every transaction posts once, to one source of truth.

## Core ERP Modules Included

- Financial management: general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, cash management, multi-entity, multi-currency.
- Inventory management: SKUs, stock locations, lot/serial tracking, reorder points, cycle counts, landed cost.
- Procurement: RFQs, purchase orders, vendor management, three-way matching, approvals.
- Sales orders & fulfillment: quote-to-cash, drop-shipping, back-orders, credit holds.
- Manufacturing: BOMs, work orders, routing, MRP, shop-floor control, capacity planning (required for any ERP system for manufacturing).
- Supply chain management: demand planning, forecasting, distribution, warehouse, logistics (full ERP for supply chain management).
- Project accounting: time, expense, WIP, revenue recognition, project billing.
- Reporting & analytics: pre-built dashboards, ad-hoc query, drill-down, export to Excel, API for BI tools.

## ERP vs Separate Business Tools — Which is Right?

If you're a 5–10 person startup, separate best-of-breed tools are usually fine. Xero or QuickBooks for accounting, HubSpot for CRM, a simple inventory tool. The integration tax is low because the data volumes are low.

The tipping point is typically between 25 and 75 employees. At that size, the cost of integrating, maintaining, and reconciling separate tools starts to exceed the cost of a proper ERP. Symptoms you've hit the tipping point: finance is spending more than three days a month on reconciliation, you've got at least one spreadsheet that nobody is allowed to edit because "it's the master," or you've tried to hire an ERP analyst on Linkedin in the past year.

### What is ERP software?

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) software is an integrated business application that manages finance, inventory, procurement, manufacturing, supply chain, and reporting from a single shared database. It replaces disconnected point tools with one system of record, so every department sees the same data in real time.

### How much does ERP software cost?

Cloud ERP software typically costs $49–$199 per user per month, depending on tier (small business, mid-size, enterprise) and module scope. Implementation adds a one-time cost of roughly 0.8x–1.5x of the first-year subscription. Artiflex IT ERP starts at $49 per user per month for small business, $99 for mid-size.

### Which ERP is best for small business?

The best ERP for small business combines fast deployment (4–6 weeks), SMB-friendly pricing (under $100 per user per month), and no need for a full-time ERP administrator. It should include financials, inventory, and basic procurement out-of-the-box, and let you add manufacturing, CRM, or HR modules later without re-implementing.

### What's the difference between cloud ERP and on-premise ERP?

Cloud ERP software runs on the vendor's servers and is accessed over the internet — lower upfront cost, automatic upgrades, better scalability. On-premise ERP runs on your own servers — higher upfront cost and IT overhead, but full data control. 95% of new ERP deployments in 2024 were cloud-based (Gartner, 2024).

### How long does ERP implementation take?

Typical ERP implementation timelines: 4–6 weeks for small business with 2–3 modules, 3–5 months for mid-size companies with full financial and operational scope, and 6–12 months for complex multi-entity enterprise deployments. Data migration and integration count drive timeline more than user count.

### Do I need custom ERP or will standard work?

Roughly 80% of business processes fit standard ERP with configuration alone — no code required. Custom ERP solutions are warranted when you run genuinely unique workflows, industry-specific compliance requirements, or proprietary business logic that creates competitive advantage. For everything else, configure the standard product.

### What is the best ERP for manufacturing?

The best ERP system for manufacturing includes a real MRP engine, multi-level BOM support, finite-capacity scheduling, work order tracking, lot and serial traceability, and engineering change order management. It should also support your production mode — discrete, process, mixed-mode, or engineer-to-order.

### Can ERP replace my CRM, HR, and accounting software?

Modern all-in-one ERP platforms include CRM, HR, and accounting modules as part of the suite. Whether you should replace existing best-of-breed tools depends on how deeply you're using them. If you're using 20% of HubSpot's features, an ERP-native CRM is usually sufficient. If you're running sophisticated marketing automation, you may keep HubSpot and integrate.
