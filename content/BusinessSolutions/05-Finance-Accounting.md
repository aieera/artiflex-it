### What is accounting software for business?

Accounting software for business is a financial management application that handles general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, invoicing, expense management, and financial reporting. Modern cloud accounting software is typically subscription-based, accessible from any device, and integrates with banking, payroll, and operational systems.

### How much does finance management software cost?

Finance management software typically costs $25–$200 per user per month. Artiflex IT pricing: small business accounting starts at $25/user/month, mid-market finance management at $79/user/month, and enterprise multi-entity finance at $149+/user/month. Implementation ranges from $5,000 for SMB to $100,000+ for multi-entity enterprise deployments.

### What's the difference between accounting software and ERP?

Accounting software focuses on financial transactions — general ledger, AP, AR, reporting. ERP includes accounting plus operational modules — inventory, procurement, manufacturing, supply chain. An ERP accounting module is the accounting functionality within an ERP, sharing a database with the operational modules so transactions flow automatically into the GL.

### Is cloud accounting software secure?

Yes, when the vendor maintains SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and bank-grade encryption (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest). Cloud accounting software is typically more secure than on-premise accounting because vendors invest more in security than most small or mid-sized IT departments can. Artiflex IT holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications.

### Can accounting software handle multiple currencies?

Yes. Multi-currency accounting software handles daily rate feeds, realized and unrealized FX gain/loss, automatic revaluation, intercompany elimination, and consolidated reporting in a chosen reporting currency. Proper multi-currency support is essential for any company selling or purchasing across borders, or operating subsidiaries in multiple countries.

### How does automated bookkeeping software work?

Automated bookkeeping software uses rules, machine learning, and bank feeds to categorize transactions, post journal entries, reconcile accounts, and close books with minimal manual input. For very small businesses it can replace a part-time bookkeeper; for larger companies it accelerates close by 40–60% while keeping human oversight for exceptions.

### What is accounts payable automation?

Accounts payable automation replaces manual bill processing with OCR-based vendor bill capture, three-way matching against POs and receipts, rule-based approval workflow, and batch payment runs. AP automation typically reduces invoice processing cost from $15–$20 per invoice to $2–$4 and cuts processing time from days to hours.

### When should I switch from QuickBooks to a full ERP?

The typical tipping points are: more than $10M in annual revenue, more than 50 employees, multiple legal entities, inventory that requires lot or serial tracking, manufacturing with BOMs, or a month-end close taking more than 10 business days. At those points, the reconciliation and workaround cost of QuickBooks exceeds the cost of a proper ERP accounting module.
