## What is HRMS Software?

HRMS (Human Resource Management System) software is an integrated application that manages the full employee lifecycle — recruitment, onboarding, core HR data, payroll, time and attendance, leave, benefits, performance, and offboarding — from a single cloud platform. It replaces the typical stack of spreadsheets, separate payroll provider, standalone time-tracking tool, and paper leave forms with one unified employee record.

## Core HR Management Software Modules

- Core HR: employee master data, org chart, reporting lines, compensation history, document store.
- Payroll management software: multi-jurisdiction payroll, statutory deductions, direct deposit, payslip distribution, year-end filings.
- Leave management system: leave types, accrual rules, approval workflow, team calendar, carry-forward, comp-off.
- Attendance tracking software: biometric, mobile, GPS, web, or kiosk-based clock in/out, overtime calculation.
- Employee onboarding software: digital offer letters, e-signatures, document collection, task lists, buddy assignment.
- Performance management system: goals, check-ins, 360 feedback, reviews, calibration, succession.
- Recruitment management software: job postings, applicant tracking, interview scheduling, offer management.
- Self-service HR portal: employees update info, request leave, access payslips; managers approve and review.
- HR compliance software: audit logs, regulatory reporting, data privacy controls, document retention.

### What is the difference between HRMS and HRIS?

HRIS (Human Resource Information System) traditionally refers to systems focused on record-keeping — employee data, reporting, compliance. HRMS (Human Resource Management System) adds transactional modules — payroll, time, leave, performance. In modern usage the terms overlap significantly; most cloud HR platforms sold today are HRMS by either definition.

### Which HR software is best for small business?

The best HR software for small business combines essential HR, payroll, leave, and attendance in one platform priced under $10 per employee per month, with deployment in under two weeks and no dedicated HR admin required. It should scale into a full HRMS as the company grows rather than forcing a re-implementation at 100 employees.

### Can HR software handle payroll?

Yes. Modern HRMS software includes a built-in payroll management module with statutory deductions, direct deposit, payslip distribution, and year-end tax form generation. Unified payroll-HR systems eliminate the data sync issues that plague setups where payroll runs on a separate provider like ADP or Gusto while HR runs on a different platform.

### What is a self-service HR portal?

A self-service HR portal lets employees manage their own HR interactions — updating personal info, requesting leave, downloading payslips, acknowledging policies — without emailing HR. Manager self-service extends this to approvals and team reports. Self-service typically reduces HR's transactional workload by 40–60%.

### How does attendance tracking software work?

Attendance tracking software captures employee clock-in and clock-out events through biometric devices, mobile apps with GPS geofencing, web timesheets, or kiosks. It calculates regular hours, overtime, night shift premiums, and exceptions (late, missed punch), then posts results directly to payroll without manual entry.

### Is cloud HR software GDPR compliant?

Yes, when the vendor maintains ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certifications and provides data subject request workflows, encryption at rest and in transit, data residency options, and configurable retention policies.
