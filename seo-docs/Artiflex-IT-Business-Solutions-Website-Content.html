What is ERP Software?

Enterprise resource planning software is an integrated application that manages a company's core business processes — finance, procurement, inventory, manufacturing, supply chain, and human resources — from a single shared database. Before ERP, each function ran on its own software, and month-end close was an act of reconciliation. With a modern cloud ERP, the numbers tie out in real time because every transaction posts once, to one source of truth.

Core ERP Modules Included

  • Financial management: general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, cash management, multi-entity, multi-currency.
  • Inventory management: SKUs, stock locations, lot/serial tracking, reorder points, cycle counts, landed cost.
  • Procurement: RFQs, purchase orders, vendor management, three-way matching, approvals.
  • Sales orders & fulfillment: quote-to-cash, drop-shipping, back-orders, credit holds.
  • Manufacturing: BOMs, work orders, routing, MRP, shop-floor control, capacity planning (required for any ERP system for manufacturing).
  • Supply chain management: demand planning, forecasting, distribution, warehouse, logistics (full ERP for supply chain management).
  • Project accounting: time, expense, WIP, revenue recognition, project billing.
  • Reporting & analytics: pre-built dashboards, ad-hoc query, drill-down, export to Excel, API for BI tools.
