# Google Business Profile Setup — Artiflex IT

**Why this is the single highest-ROI SEO move for Artiflex.** UAE B2B buyers vet IT vendors through Google Maps before clicking through to a website. For every Dubai-geo query like "cybersecurity company dubai" or "IT AMC dubai", Google shows a 3-result local pack (Map + listings) above the 10 blue links. If Artiflex doesn't rank in that pack, the first organic result is below the fold on mobile — which is where most UAE search happens.

Free to set up. Takes 60–90 minutes for initial creation + optimization. Listing becomes visible within 3–14 days after verification.

---

## Step 1 — Create or claim the listing

1. Go to https://business.google.com and sign in with a company email (not personal Gmail). Ideally use a Workspace account that's already on `artiflexit.com` — it shortcuts domain verification.
2. Search for "Artiflex Information Technology" first. If a listing already exists (someone may have created one informally), claim it. If not, click **Add your business to Google**.
3. Business name: **Artiflex Information Technology** (exactly as registered — not "Artiflex IT" unless that's the trade name on the UAE trade license. Name must match the license to pass verification).

## Step 2 — Categories (CRITICAL — this is the main ranking signal)

Primary category controls which searches trigger your listing. Pick ONE primary and add 4–9 additional.

**Primary:**
- **Information Technology Company** — matches the broadest UAE B2B intent.

**Additional categories (add all that apply):**
- Computer Security Service
- Computer Consultant
- Computer Support and Services
- Software Company
- Network Engineer
- Cloud Service Provider (if available in your region)
- Business Management Consultant

Do NOT add categories that don't match the business — Google penalizes mismatched categories and it can suspend the listing.

## Step 3 — Location

**If you have a public-facing Dubai office**, enter the full address. Verification is via postcard or Google Workspace domain match.

**If the office is staff-only / not walk-in**, set it as a **Service-area business (SAB)**:
- In the Location section, select "I deliver goods and services to my customers."
- Uncheck "I also serve customers at my business address" to hide the street address.
- In **Service areas**, add the emirates/cities you serve: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Fujairah, Ras Al Khaimah, Umm Al Quwain. Add Muscat, Riyadh if you want those included (but they'll weaken your UAE ranking — consider separate listings if you have physical presence there).

## Step 4 — Contact + website

- Phone: UAE landline or +971 mobile. This number must answer in business hours — Google occasionally calls. Toll-free 800 numbers are fine.
- Website: `https://artiflexit.com` (non-www, matches canonical).
- Appointment link: `https://artiflexit.com/contact` (shows as a "Book" button — good conversion booster).

## Step 5 — Hours

Sun–Thu business hours matching your actual schedule (UAE standard is typically 09:00–18:00 Sun–Thu). Mark Fri/Sat as closed or weekend hours. Include Ramadan/UAE public holidays as "special hours" each year.

In the **Services** section, add each service with a custom description. Google indexes these descriptions and uses them for relevance matching. Minimum list to add:

1. **Cybersecurity Services** — "NGFW, EDR, SASE, SIEM, MDR, VAPT. Platinum Sophos partner. NESA, SAMA, ISO 27001 aligned."
2. **Cloud Solutions** — "Cloud migration, IaaS, PaaS, SaaS across AWS, Azure, and private cloud."

4. **Managed IT Services** — "24/7 monitoring, help desk, patch management, incident response."
5. **Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)** — "Preventive maintenance, 24/7 monitoring, backup testing, hardware lifecycle management."
6. **ERP Software Implementation** — "Cloud and on-premise ERP — finance, inventory, procurement, supply chain."
7. **CRM Software Implementation** — "Sales automation, lead management, customer service on one platform."

9. **Application Security** — "VAPT, API testing, WAF deployment, code review."
10. **Disaster Recovery** — "Business continuity planning, DR site design, backup strategy."

Each service becomes a URL-linkable page on your listing that can deep-link to the corresponding `/cybersecurity`, `/managed-services` etc. page.

## Step 7 — Products (optional but valuable)

For each Business Solution product (ERP, CRM, etc.), add a "Product" entry with name, 1–2 sentence description, price range ("Starting from $19/user/month"), and a deep-link URL to the corresponding `/business-solutions/*` page.

## Step 9 — Photos (ranking factor + CTR booster)

Listings with 100+ photos get ~520% more calls than listings with <10. Upload:

- **Logo** — 720×720, transparent PNG
- **Cover** — 1080×608, office exterior or hero team shot
- **Team** — 5–10 photos of engineers, ops, leadership
- **Office interior** — conference room, desks, SOC if you have one (huge credibility signal for cybersecurity buyers)
- **Certifications** — Sophos Platinum Partner badge, NESA, ISO 27001, Microsoft partner, Cisco, Fortinet
- **Work photos** — server racks being installed, cabling, data center work (stock is OK if real photos are limited but real is better)
- **Client logos** — with permission, create 1 composite image of 6–9 notable client logos

Refresh with 1–2 new photos per month. Recency is a minor ranking signal.

## Step 10 — Reviews (the #1 local-pack ranking factor)

Target: **30 reviews with ≥4.5 average rating in the first 90 days.** This gets you into the local pack for most UAE "[service] dubai" queries.

**How to get them without incentivizing** (which is against Google TOS):

1. **Generate a review link** in your GBP dashboard → Share. Looks like `https://g.page/r/...`.
2. **After every successful project handoff**, the account manager sends a plain-text follow-up:
   > "[Client first name], it's been a pleasure working with you on the [project name] deployment. If you have two minutes, would you share your experience on our Google profile? It helps other UAE businesses find us. [short link]. No worries if you're slammed — we appreciate your trust either way."
3. **Prioritize happy long-term clients.** Aim for 5–10 reviews in week 1 from people you know will leave strong ones.
4. **Respond to every review within 48 hours.** Thank positive reviews by name, address negative reviews professionally (Google's algorithm weights response rate).
5. **Never buy reviews or use review-gating services.** Detected = suspension. Permanent.

## Step 11 — Q&A (pre-populate)

Google's Q&A section lets anyone ask a question publicly — including competitors. Pre-populate with 5–8 questions you want answered your way:

Seed these yourself from a personal Google account (from a different IP if possible):

1. "Does Artiflex IT support AMC for SMBs in Dubai?" — Yes, answer with AMC starting price + link.
2. "What cybersecurity certifications does Artiflex hold?" — Sophos Platinum, ISO 27001 aligned, etc.
3. "Do you provide on-site support across the UAE?" — Yes, cover Dubai/AD/Sharjah on-site, remote across UAE.
4. "Is Artiflex an authorized Fortinet/Palo Alto/Cisco partner?" — List active vendor partnerships.
5. "What's the response SLA for managed services?" — 15 min P1, 2hr P2, etc.
6. "Do you offer free security assessments?" — Yes, 30-min consultation, link to `/contact`.
7. "What industries do you serve?" — Banking, government, healthcare, retail, manufacturing in UAE.
8. "Do you handle NESA / SAMA compliance?" — Yes, NIST CSF 2.0-aligned roadmaps.

## Step 12 — Posts (weekly cadence)

Every 7 days, publish a Google Post (max 1,500 chars + image + CTA). Types that perform best for B2B IT:

- **Update** — new certification, vendor partnership, office expansion
- **Event** — webinar, conference appearance (GITEX, LEAP, etc.)
- **Offer** — "Free UAE cybersecurity assessment — book April"
- **What's new** — new service, case study published

Posts expire after 7 days, but they influence rankings during their lifespan and they prove to Google that the profile is actively maintained.

## Step 13 — UTM tagging for analytics

Append UTM to the website URL so GA4 credits GBP traffic correctly:

```
https://artiflexit.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp
```

Do the same on the appointment link and each service deep-link. Otherwise GBP clicks show as "google / organic" and mix with regular search.

## Step 14 — Verify + publish

Google requires business verification. Methods (in order of speed):

1. **Workspace domain** — if your GBP owner is on `@artiflexit.com` Workspace, Google may auto-verify. Fastest.
2. **Video verification** — Google prompts a short video of you at the office with signage + streetview visible. Usually approved in 3–5 business days.
3. **Postcard to address** — 10–14 days to UAE. Only if other methods aren't offered.

Don't enter the verification code in any form other than the one inside Google Business Profile. Scam emails impersonating Google about verification are common — ignore them.

## Step 15 — Monitor (weekly cadence after launch)

Watch these four metrics in GBP Insights:

- **Search queries** — what people typed to find you. Feeds keyword targeting for website content.
- **Search appearances by type** — "Direct" (searched your name) vs "Discovery" (searched a category). Discovery is the growth signal; direct is brand.
- **Customer actions** — calls, direction requests, website clicks. Set baseline in week 2.
- **Photo views** — trends up if recency is maintained.

Track month-over-month. Target 20% MoM growth on Discovery searches for the first 6 months.
